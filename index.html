FAQ

Questions? We got you.

Is RizzUp really 100% free?
+

Yes, completely. No hidden fees, no premium tier, no credit card required. Every feature — including the AI coach, translator, and dialogue — is free. The only requirement for AI features is an internet connection.

Does it work without internet?
+

Yes! Install RizzUp as a PWA (Progressive Web App) and all the core features work offline: word explorer, pronunciation coach, games, challenges, minimal pairs, and daily review. Only the AI Coach, Translator, and Dialogue need an internet connection.

How do I install it on my phone?
+

Open RizzUp in your mobile browser, then tap the "Share" button (iOS) or the menu (Android) and select "Add to Home Screen". It installs like a native app — no App Store or Play Store needed. On desktop, look for the install icon in your browser's address bar.

What level do I need to start?
+

Any level! RizzUp has content from A1 (absolute beginner) to C1 (advanced). You can filter all words, lessons, and exercises by level. Start wherever you feel comfortable.

Which AI powers the coach and translator?
+

RizzUp uses Claude by Anthropic — one of the most capable and safe AI models available. It provides corrections, explanations, and contextual translations in real-time.

Does RizzUp teach formal English too?
+

Absolutely. While RizzUp is famous for teaching slang and informal speech, the word explorer covers all registers — from casual Gen Z slang to professional phrasal verbs and formal idioms. The AI translator even tells you the register of every translation.

Can I use RizzUp for a job interview?
+

Yes! The "Job Interview" dialogue scenario specifically trains you for professional English conversations. The AI will correct your grammar, suggest more natural phrasing, and help you sound confident and natural.
